Written by: Isabelle Romualdez We at iPrice Group regularly come out with a Map of E-commerce Report wherein we determine the rankings of E-commerce players in Southeast Asia. These reports have always garnered us many publications and references from the top media in the region. However, getting media coverage for periodic reports can be quite tricky as journalists are always in the lookout for something new.
