# iPrice Group

> iPrice Group is a Southeast Asian e-commerce aggregator and price-comparison platform headquartered in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia. It aggregates billions of product offers and thousands of coupons from more than a thousand retail partners across seven markets, helping shoppers compare prices and find deals. iPrice does not publish a public developer API; this profile is a company record in the API Evangelist network.

## Company
- [iPrice Group website](https://ipricegroup.com): Corporate site describing the price-comparison and coupon platform.
- [Careers](https://ipricegroup.com/career.html): Open roles across seven Southeast Asian markets.
- [Blog (Medium)](https://ipricegroup.medium.com/): Company engineering and e-commerce insights blog.

Malaysia, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam, Philippines, Hong Kong.
